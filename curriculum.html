Gratitude and Unique Curriculum

A beautiful bridge between story and SEL

This curriculum companion brings Social Emotional Learning to life through intentional conversations, gratitude practices, and confidence-building activities.

Children explore what makes them unique, recognize their strengths, appreciate the qualities they bring to the world, and develop a deeper sense of self-awareness and belonging.

  • A thoughtfully sequenced two-week learning experience for classroom schedules and home learning
  • Developmentally appropriate activities supporting gratitude, identity, confidence, kindness, and belonging
  • Hands-on experiences that help children reflect, create, connect, and celebrate unique qualities
  • Standards-aligned practices supporting early childhood learning goals and Social Emotional Learning
  • Classroom workshops and author readings that bring the story directly to children and educators
